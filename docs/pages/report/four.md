## Navigation and Understanding
Are you stuck at a point without knowing what to do to move or make an exit? Links provide the perfect relief and exit plan.

Navigation and movement is an important aspect of every human life and so it is for every website and webpage. Users want to be able to move from one part of the website to another such as from **Home** to **Contact us** seemlessly or even to another website entirely.

Also too much information can be difficult to grasp if they are text based without any supporting images to provide an idea of what is being communicated.

Links can be in two forms with one being full and the other being embedded and can be easily accessed by clicking on them.. i.e https://www.nasa.gov or [NASA's Website](https://www.nasa.gov) and they are both valid links leading to the same website.

Embedded links are written in a markdown in this way **`[Link](https://url)`**

# What Are Images?

![UWM Logo](https://uwm.edu/brand/wp-content/uploads/sites/610/2020/11/uwm-seo-logo.jpg)

An **image** displays a picture inside your Markdown document.

The syntax looks very similar to a link but begins with an exclamation mark (`!`).

**`![Alternative Text](image.jpg)`** or **`![Alternative Text](http://url)`**


# Best Practices

✅ Use descriptive link text.

✅ Always include meaningful alternative text for images.

✅ Only use images that are relevant to your content.

✅ Check that your links work before publishing your document.
