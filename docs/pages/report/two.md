## Making Important Ideas Stand Out

Making important text stand out in a document or write up is very crucial in order for important information not to be lost in the main text.

In order to achieve this certain text may be made unique through bolding them or italicizing them or even both. Text are made bold using two asterisks (**) and italicized using one asterisk (*)

## What Are Bold and Italics?

Markdown allows you to emphasize text in two common ways:

- **Bold** makes text stand out.
- *Italics* gently emphasize words or phrases.

| Markdown | Result |
|----------|--------|
| `**Bold**` | **Bold** |
| `*Italic*` | *Italic* |
| `***Bold and Italic***` | ***Bold and Italic*** |

# Best Practices

✅ Use **bold** only for important information.

✅ Use *italics* for gentle emphasis or special terms.

✅ Avoid making entire paragraphs bold or italic.

✅ Use emphasis consistently throughout your document.
